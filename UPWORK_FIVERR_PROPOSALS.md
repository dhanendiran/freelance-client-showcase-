Never waste those two lines on pleasantries like *"Dear Hiring Manager, I am a software engineer with 5 years experience..."*. Hook them immediately with the solution.

## Upwork Filter Strategy (To Get Jobs in 2 Hours)
When searching jobs on Upwork, set these exact filters:
1. **Search Query:** `Landing page` OR `Figma to HTML` OR `Fix CSS` OR `Website redesign`
2. **Experience Level:** Entry Level or Intermediate
3. **Job Type:** Fixed Price ($50 - $300)
4. **Client Info:** Payment Verified, 4+ stars
5. **Sort by:** **Newest / Less than 2 hours old**
6. *Rule of thumb:* Submit proposals within 15–30 minutes of a job being posted. If a job has 20–50 proposals already, move to the next one!
