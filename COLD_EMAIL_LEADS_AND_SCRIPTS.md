# 🎯 Cold Outreach Playbook (Local Businesses — Fastest Cash)

Local businesses (dentists, plumbers, electricians, roofing contractors, auto detailers, gyms, MedSpas) make hundreds or thousands of dollars per customer. Paying $200–$400 for a modern website that gets them 1 extra call per month is a no-brainer for them.

---

## Step 1: Finding 20 Leads in 10 Minutes (Free via Google Maps)

1. Open **Google Maps** (https://maps.google.com).
2. Pick any mid-sized city (e.g. Austin, Tampa, Denver, Manchester, Sydney, or your local metro).
3. Search for service businesses:
   * `"dentist in [city]"`
   * `"roofing contractor in [city]"`
   * `"plumber in [city]"`
   * `"auto detailing in [city]"`
   * `"landscaping in [city]"`
4. Click on businesses in the results and look at their website link:
   * **Target Type A (Goldmine):** "No website" listed in Google Maps. (They lose 50%+ of potential customers).
   * **Target Type B (High Value):** Website exists, but looks outdated, text is tiny on mobile, or takes 6+ seconds to load.
5. Grab their contact info:
   * Their email address (found on their "Contact" or "About" page, or footer).
   * Or their Instagram / Facebook page link.
   * Or send a quick message through their existing contact form.

---
